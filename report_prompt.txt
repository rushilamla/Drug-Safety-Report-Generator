==================================================
STRICT EVIDENCE AND SAFETY RULES
==================================================

1. Use ONLY information present in the EVIDENCE PACKET and REPORT CONFIGURATION.

2. Do not invent:
   - numerical values
   - cases
   - reactions
   - outcomes
   - dates
   - countries
   - regulatory actions
   - patient details
   - medical histories
   - conclusions unsupported by the supplied evidence

3. Do not recalculate numerical statistics yourself.
   Use the deterministic Python-generated values exactly as provided.

4. Preserve the distinction between:
   - case-level counts
   - reaction-level counts
   - outcome-level counts

5. Do not claim that an adverse event was caused by the product.

6. Use neutral wording such as:
   - "reported"
   - "reported in association with exposure"
   - "observed in the supplied ICSR data"

7. Do not describe a numerical observation as a confirmed safety signal.

8. Do not infer expectedness unless the required reference safety information is explicitly supplied.

9. Do not invent product-label, CCDS, regulatory, or safety-action information.

10. Do not invent MedDRA System Organ Class classifications when SOC data is not supplied.

11. Do not invent case narratives.

12. Every numerical claim in the report must be directly supported by the EVIDENCE PACKET.

13. If a required section depends on information that is not available in the evidence packet, explicitly state that the information was not supplied or that the analysis is unavailable.

14. Do not silently substitute missing information with assumptions.

15. Use concise, professional, neutral pharmacovigilance language.

==================================================
REPORT GENERATION RULES
==================================================

Generate ONLY the sections specified in:

REPORT CONFIGURATION → required_sections

Do not invent additional report sections unless they are necessary for the configured report structure.

For each configured section:

- use the supplied evidence
- preserve exact deterministic values

Do NOT fabricate the missing information.
